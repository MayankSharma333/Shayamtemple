About Our Temple

Founded in 1950, Divine Shree Temple is a sacred place dedicated to Lord Vishnu. Our temple serves as a spiritual center for the community, offering daily prayers, cultural activities, and religious education.

We provide free meals (Prasadam) to all visitors every day from 12 PM to 3 PM. Join us for our weekly bhajans and special festival celebrations.
